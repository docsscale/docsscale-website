## What you need to know first

- **One primary keyword per page, and no two pages share one.** The table in
  section 2 covers every existing page and the seven planned service pages.
- **Four of the seven service pages have no keyword with data yet.** Keyword
  Planner returned nothing usable for websites, lead follow-up, reviews and
  reactivation. Section 6 lists the seeds for one more export.
- **The numbers are ranges, not counts.** Google gave this account three bands:
  10–100, 100–1K and 1K–10K searches a month in the United States. A term that
  isn't in an export is written **Unknown**.
- **Search Console can't help yet.** In the last 90 days the whole site had 14
  impressions on the homepage and 1 on `/book-a-call/`, and Google reported no
  queries. Every "current position" is therefore **Insufficient data**.
- **The quickest wins are specific posts, not the main pages.** The head terms
  ("healthcare marketing agency", "dental marketing agency") are held by
  national and specialist agencies. Section 3 lists the long-tail topics where
  the current results are weaker.
- **City pages are not decided here.** They wait for your extra Texas export
  (section 5).

**Two terms I recommend against, with the reason:**

- **"missed call text back"** (100–1K). It looks like a fit for lead follow-up,
  but people searching it want software instructions: the top result is the CRM
  platform's own help site and Google's suggested questions name that platform.
  Targeting it would pull the page toward the platform you don't name on the
  main site.
- **"healthcare lead generation agency"** (10–100). The results are companies
  that sell appointment-setting to medical suppliers, not marketing for
  clinics. Wrong audience.

## 4. Overlaps to watch

- **Dental.** "dental marketing agency" (industry page), "dental marketing
  cost" and "how to choose a dental marketing agency" (articles) are close.
  They are kept apart by intent: hire, price, compare. Each must link to the
  other two.
- **Industry page versus "ideas" posts.** `/industries/med-spa/` and "med spa
  marketing ideas" must not cover the same ground: the page is about what we
  do, the post about what a clinic can do itself.
- **Reactivation page versus the recall-messages post.** Same split: the
  service, and a how-to with real message examples.
- **Local SEO page versus the Google Business Profile post.** The post is one
  part of the service, in depth, for dentists only.
