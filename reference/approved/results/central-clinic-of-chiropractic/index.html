What chiropractors ask us.

How do chiropractors get more new patients?

Three sources that feed each other: ads that reach people in pain near you, a system that answers and books every enquiry, and finished patients who refer. At Central Clinic all three run at once.

How much should a chiropractor spend on ads?

Central Clinic spends around $3k a month on Meta and Google. The budget matters less than what happens after the click: an enquiry nobody answers is an ad wasted.

How long does chiropractic marketing take to work?

Here, the first three months were slow, and month four is when referrals and walk-ins started stacking on top of the ads. Judge a system over a year, not a month.

Does the clinic still talk to patients itself?

Whenever it wants to. The system books, reminds and follows up on its own, staff call the patients they choose to, and a receptionist line covers the phone when they are busy.
