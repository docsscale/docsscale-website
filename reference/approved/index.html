Local SEO

Google Business Profile for dentists: what to fix first

Your Google Business Profile is what most new patients see before your website. Here is what to fix first, in the order that matters, and what you can safely ignore.

8 October 2026 · 5 min read
Questions

What clinic owners ask us first.

Something missing? Email info@docsscale.com and a person answers.

Do you only work with one kind of clinic?
No. Dental, chiropractic, physical therapy, med spa, weight loss, dermatology, primary care, optometry, and mental health. The system is the same; offers, timing, and compliance rules change by specialty.
We already have a website. Do we have to rebuild it?
Only if it can't book. If it can, we build funnels around it. If it can't, we fix that first, because ads pointed at a site that doesn't convert waste your money.
Who pays for and owns the ad spend?
You pay the platform directly from your own ad account. We manage it, you own it, and the account, pages, and numbers stay yours if we ever part ways.
How quickly will we see new patients?
Most clinics see their first booked patients from ads within 2–3 weeks of launch and a full pipeline by month three.
Are we locked into a contract?
Month to month, 30 days' notice. Clinics stay because the schedule fills, not because a contract says so.
How do you handle patient information?
We sign a BAA with every clinic and never store clinical records; contact details live in your own accounts. The minimum data needed, never sold or shared.
